  OG IMAGE — REQUIRED FOR WHATSAPP PREVIEW CARD
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

When someone shares your website link on WhatsApp, it shows
a preview card with an image. This image is: og-image.jpg

WHAT TO CREATE:
  File: public/images/og-image.jpg
  Size: 1200 × 630 pixels (exact, very important)
  Format: JPG, max 300 KB

WHAT IT SHOULD LOOK LIKE:
  - Dark background (#0f0f0f or similar)
  - Your AMSO logo centered or top-left
  - Tagline: "Construction Company · Guanacaste, Costa Rica"
  - A beautiful construction/architecture photo in the background
  - Orange accent color (#F97316)

QUICK OPTION — Use Canva (free):
  1. Go to canva.com → Create → Custom size → 1200 × 630 px
  2. Dark background
  3. Add your logo
  4. Add text: "Grupo AMSO | Construction in Guanacaste, Costa Rica"
  5. Download as JPG
  6. Save as: public/images/og-image.jpg

AFTER UPLOADING:
  Test your WhatsApp preview at:
  https://developers.facebook.com/tools/debug/
  (enter your website URL and click "Scrape Again")

NOTE: WhatsApp caches OG images aggressively.
If you change the image, use the debugger above to clear the cache.
